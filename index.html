CampusAssist (concept prototype)

AI-assisted student help desk and instant practice-quiz feedback

CIT1142 Emerging Trends in ICT · Manoah Mwansa Chama (202500902) · Lecturer: Alex Ng'uni

Prototype notice: a JavaScript NLP engine (TF-IDF retrieval + Naive Bayes sensitivity classifier) selects answers from approved public ZCAS information and shows its confidence. It is not a generative AI model. Low-confidence or sensitive questions become a ticket for a human.

Evaluation. Set 1 is the report test; Sets 2 and 3 use reworded questions. Set 3 was written after tuning. Expected outcomes were fixed before running.

Knowledge base: eight entries from public ZCAS website pages (zcas.ac.zm). Staff must verify them before real use. The known failure (question 7) is explained in Section 5 of the report.
